Superior Court, County of San Diego, Department 14
People v. Jordan Thomas Whitfield, Case No. CD-2026-104582
Change of Plea Hearing, August 21, 2026

Appearances:
Deputy District Attorney M. Castillo, for the People
Deputy Public Defender A. Brennan, for the Defendant

THE COURT: We're on the record in People versus Whitfield. Mr. Whitfield is present, in custody, with counsel. I understand there's a resolution.

MS. BRENNAN: Yes, Your Honor. Mr. Whitfield will be entering a plea of guilty to Count 1, second-degree commercial burglary under Penal Code section 459. The People will move to dismiss Count 2, vandalism, with a Harvey waiver as to restitution.

MS. CASTILLO: That's correct, Your Honor. There is no agreement as to sentence. The People will be requesting full restitution to the victim.

THE COURT: Mr. Whitfield, have you had enough time to talk with your attorney about this plea?

THE DEFENDANT: Yes, Your Honor.

THE COURT: Has anyone promised you anything or threatened you to get you to plead guilty?

THE DEFENDANT: No.

THE COURT: To Count 1, a violation of Penal Code section 459, second-degree burglary, how do you plead?

THE DEFENDANT: Guilty.

THE COURT: Is there anything you would like to say?

THE DEFENDANT: Just that I'm sorry for what I did to the store. I lost my job and I panicked about rent, but that's not an excuse. I want to pay them back.

THE COURT: The Court finds the plea knowing, intelligent, and voluntary. The matter is referred to the Probation Department for a pre-sentence report. Sentencing is set for October 23, 2026, in this department. The defendant is remanded.

(Proceedings concluded.)
