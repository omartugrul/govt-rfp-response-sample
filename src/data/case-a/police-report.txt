INCIDENT REPORT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

Agency: Sheriff's Department, Lakeside Substation
Report No.: 26-118473
Date of Incident: June 18, 2026, approx. 0215 hours
Location: Mesa Ridge Hardware, 2210 Mesa Ridge Boulevard
Reporting Deputy: Deputy R. Alvarez #4471
Offense: PC 459, Burglary (commercial)
Suspect: Jordan Thomas Whitfield, DOB 03/14/1995

NARRATIVE

On June 18, 2026, at approximately 0215 hours, I was dispatched to Mesa Ridge Hardware regarding an audible burglary alarm. Upon arrival at 0221 hours, I observed that the glass panel of the rear service door had been broken and the door was ajar.

While checking the rear lot, I observed a male subject walking away from the building carrying a large duffel bag. I identified myself and ordered the subject to stop. The subject complied, set the bag on the ground, and stated, "I'm not going to run."

The subject was identified by California driver's license as Jordan Thomas Whitfield. Inside the duffel bag I located two cordless drill kits, one cordless circular saw, one reciprocating saw, and four lithium battery packs, all bearing Mesa Ridge Hardware inventory tags. Whitfield had a small cut on his right hand, and a pair of work gloves was found in his jacket pocket.

Whitfield was advised of his Miranda rights and stated he understood them. Whitfield stated he had been "out of work for a couple months" and was "going to sell the tools to cover rent." He stated he broke the glass with a rock and entered the store alone. He stated he had not used drugs or alcohol that night. I did not observe objective signs of intoxication.

Store manager Patricia Nguyen arrived at 0305 hours and reviewed the recovered property. Nguyen stated the recovered tools had a retail value of approximately $1,840. Nguyen also identified that one additional circular saw and one tool battery were missing from the display and were not recovered, with a combined retail value of approximately $410. Nguyen estimated the cost to replace the rear door glass at approximately $310.

Store security video showed a single male matching Whitfield's description entering through the rear door at 0209 hours and exiting at 0217 hours. No other subjects were observed.

Whitfield was transported to the Las Colinas detention facility and booked for PC 459. The recovered property was photographed and returned to Nguyen at the scene.

Deputy R. Alvarez #4471
