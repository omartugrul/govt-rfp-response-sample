PROBATION CASE NOTES (FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

Case: People v. Jordan Thomas Whitfield, CD-2026-104582
Assigned Officer: PO J. Ramirez

09/02/2026: Defendant interview (Las Colinas, in person, 55 min)

Defendant is 31 years old. He was born and raised in El Cajon and is the younger of two children. His parents divorced when he was 9; he lived primarily with his mother, who passed away in 2023. He reported a close relationship with his older sister, Denise Whitfield.

Defendant graduated from Granite Hills High School in 2013 and completed a 9-month HVAC technician certificate program at a community college in 2016.

Defendant worked as an HVAC installer for Coastline Climate Services from 2017 until April 2026, when he was laid off during a staff reduction. He reported he had not found steady work since and had fallen two months behind on rent at the time of the offense.

Defendant has a 4-year-old daughter who lives with her mother in Santee. He reported he paid informal child support while employed and sees his daughter most weekends.

Defendant reported he began drinking alcohol at 16. He reported drinking heavily in his early twenties, which he connected to his 2017 DUI arrest. He completed a first-offender DUI program in 2018. He reported current drinking of "a few beers on weekends" and denied drinking to intoxication in the past year. He reported occasional methamphetamine use between 2018 and 2019 and denied any use since completing outpatient treatment in 2020.

Defendant stated, "I knew it was wrong when I was doing it. I was desperate and I made a bad decision." He stated he is willing to complete any program the court orders and wants to pay restitution to the store.

09/08/2026: Collateral contact, Denise Whitfield (sister), by phone

Ms. Whitfield confirmed the defendant lost his job in April and stated he "was trying hard to find work." She stated the defendant may live with her in Lakeside upon release and that she could help him get to appointments. She stated she was not aware of any current drug use.

09/10/2026: Victim contact, Patricia Nguyen (Mesa Ridge Hardware store manager), by phone

Ms. Nguyen stated the store's total loss was $720, consisting of the unrecovered circular saw and battery ($410) and the rear door glass replacement ($310). She stated the recovered tools were returned undamaged and put back on sale. She stated staff were "shaken up" and the store has since added a security gate to the rear door. She stated the store is requesting full restitution and does not wish to make a further statement to the court.
