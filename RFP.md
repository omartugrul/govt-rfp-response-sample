## Confirmed — from the RFP listing (Source 1)

- **Buyer:** County of San Diego, Probation Department (CA)
- **Title:** Artificial Intelligence (AI) Report Writing Services (Request for Proposal)
- **Posted:** Sep 1, 2026
- **Closes:** Oct 16, 2026
- **Scope (verbatim):** "design, deliver, and maintain an artificial intelligence‑enabled solution that streamlines the County of San Diego (County) Probation Department (Probation) report preparation by reducing manual workload and supporting efficient data extraction, summarization, and drafting"
- **Also stated:** maintain County oversight, security, and privacy requirements; preserve officer decision-making authority
- **Vendor/brand:** none named

## Confirmed — from news coverage of the draft contract (Source 2)

**What it's for**
- Pre-sentencing reports that probation officers prepare for judges
- ~7,800 reports per year, roughly 3 per officer per week

**Inputs the AI would summarize**
- Police reports
- Court transcripts
- Case notes
- Criminal-history records

**Timeline**
- Sep 1, 2026 — draft contract released
- Sep 8, 2026 — presented to interested vendors
- Oct 16, 2026 — proposals due

**Constraints in the draft contract**
- AI **cannot make sentencing recommendations**
- Use is **optional** for officers
- Not used for reports on **juvenile defendants** (may access juvenile records of adults)
- County data restricted to County use; no unauthorized sharing
- Vendor needs **written approval** to train AI on County data
- **Annual bias and fairness assessments** required

**Context**
- Department is short-staffed and exceeded its overtime budget in 2025
- A County Supervisor has publicly called for accuracy, equity, due process, error and bias testing, and human oversight
